Privacy Policy

How information submitted through this website is handled.

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

At Dezynvia, accessible from our official platform and services, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and clients. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Dezynvia and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through our dedicated channels.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our services, when you participate in activities on our website, or when you contact us directly.

  • Personal Data Provided by You: This includes names, phone numbers (including mobile numbers provided for WhatsApp communications), email addresses, billing addresses, company names, project briefs, asset files, and other communication preferences.
  • Automatically Collected Information: When you visit our website, our servers automatically log standard data provided by your web browser. This may include your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, the pages you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on each page.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways to maintain and optimize our services, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the functional infrastructure of our website.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our website layout and creative service offerings.
  • Understand and analyze how you interact with our web assets and project portfolios.
  • Develop new products, digital services, features, and technical workflows.
  • Communicate with you directly or through our communication integrations (such as our WhatsApp Click-to-Chat CTA links) to provide project updates, quotes, and marketing communications.
  • Process transactions, manage project scopes, and prevent fraudulent actions.

3. Log Files

Dezynvia follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this as part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Dezynvia uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

5. Third-Party Links and Integrations

Our website contains links to external platforms, including portfolio mockups, project demos, and direct WhatsApp communication gateways. Please be aware that we have no control over the content and privacy practices of these external sites and integrations. We cannot accept responsibility or liability for their respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service provider you interact with.

6. Data Security

The security of your data is of paramount importance to us. We implement commercially acceptable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. Dezynvia does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

8. Consent

By utilizing our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms and operational conditions.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, or if you would like to update any personal preferences you shared with us, please reach out to our team:

  • By using our direct website contact button.
  • By starting a direct consultation chat through our official WhatsApp button.
