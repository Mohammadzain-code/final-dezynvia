FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own Shopify account?

Yes, listings are added to your own Shopify store. Plan fees are paid directly to Shopify.

Can you handle large catalogues?

Bulk listing support is available; the scope is confirmed in your quotation.

Who writes the product descriptions?

You can supply them or ask us to format and organise your content. Writing from scratch should be confirmed first.
