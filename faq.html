Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Can't find yours? Just ask.

What services do you offer?

We offer Web Development, WordPress Development, Graphic Designing, Social Media Post Designing and Shopify Product Listing. See the services page for details.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of your project. We prepare a customised quotation after learning about your needs. See pricing.

How do payments work?

Payment arrangements are agreed in writing in your quotation before work begins.

How long will my project take?

Timelines depend on the scope and are confirmed in your quotation. We do not promise fixed delivery times before understanding the project.

How many design revisions are included?

Revision limits are set per package and stated in your quotation.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

A website needs both. They are usually separate costs unless stated in your quotation. We can advise on what you need.

Can you build or redesign a WordPress site?

Yes. We offer WordPress websites, theme customisation, landing pages and website redesigns.

Can you add products to my Shopify store?

Yes. We handle product uploads, titles, descriptions, variants, pricing entry, collections and organisation in your own Shopify store.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Updates, maintenance and ongoing support are optional services that need confirmation. Contact us to discuss your needs.

Still have questions?

Send us a message and we will be happy to help.
