Sample Article: 5 Things to Plan Before Building a Business Website

A template showing how a Dezynvia article looks.

Sample content Author: [Add author] · Date: [Add date]

This is an original sample article used as a template. Replace the headings and paragraphs with your own writing.

1. Define your goal

Decide what you want visitors to do: contact you, buy something, or book a service. Every page should support that goal.

2. List the pages you need

Start simple: Home, About, Services, Contact. Add more only when there is content to fill them.

3. Prepare your content

Gather your logo, photos, service descriptions and contact details before design begins.

4. Choose your platform

A custom-coded site, WordPress or Shopify can each suit different needs. Talk to your developer about trade-offs.

5. Plan for after launch

Domains, hosting and updates continue after launch. Include them in your budget.
